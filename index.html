My Journey

Two decades before this moment

Nothing about this work happened overnight. It's the sum of every classroom, every office, every person I ever trained, listened to, or led — pointed, finally, in one direction.

1993–1996

Kirti College, Mumbai University

Where curiosity about the human body began — botany, chemistry and zoology, long before I knew where it would lead.

1998–2004

Teacher

St. Francis’ High School, India

Pre-primary to 4th grade. Learning that patience is a skill you build one classroom at a time — not a personality trait you're born with.

St. Francis’ High School, India

Fifteen years of admissions, syllabus, hiring, events and records — and, quietly, fifteen years of building confidence in hundreds of young minds.

Chunara Food Group

Hiring new crew, training baristas, coaching customer service — and discovering how much people open up when someone simply, actually listens.

Now

Women’s Health Educator

Bringing awareness to perimenopause & menopause

Every one of those years — teaching, leading, listening — pointed toward a conversation most women were never given: what this transition actually feels like, and how to move through it well, together.

The Work I Do Now

What we talk about, in sessions

Plain language. No jargon. No judgment. Just an honest map of what's happening — and what actually helps.

01

Understanding Perimenopause

Recognising the early signs before menopause, so they stop feeling confusing, random, or frightening.

02

Menopause & the Body

Hormonal shifts, sleep, energy, temperature — and an honest picture of what's actually “normal.”

03

Mind & Mood

The emotional side of this transition — irritability, anxiety, identity — and why none of it means something is wrong with you.

04

Breaking the Silence

Replacing whispered half-truths passed between women with real, shared, out-loud understanding.

05

Everyday Tools

Simple, practical habits around sleep, movement and steadiness that support you through the transition.

06

Community Over Isolation

Because no woman should have to move through this stage feeling like she's the only one.
